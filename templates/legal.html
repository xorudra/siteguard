Privacy

Privacy Policy

SiteGuard is built to forget. Scans run in memory on our server and nothing about them is stored — no database, no scan history, no account. This page says exactly what that means.

What happens when you scan a site

You type a website address. Our server fetches that site, runs the checks, and shows you a report. The report exists only in that response and in your browser. When you close the page, it's gone — we keep no copy of the report, the findings, or the address you scanned.

Your IP address

To keep the free service fair (10 scans per hour), we hold your IP address in memory only, just long enough to count requests inside that hour. It is not written to a database, not used for tracking or advertising, and disappears when the counting window passes or the server restarts.

The name on a PoC report

If you generate a Proof of Concept report, you can type your name so it appears on the document. That name — and the report itself — travels inside a tamper-proof signed token carried by your own browser, never in storage on our server. We cannot read, list, or retrieve past reports.

Cookies and tracking

SiteGuard sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. There is nothing to consent to because there is nothing collecting.

The sites you scan

Scans are fetched from our server, so a scanned site sees our server's address — not yours. What a scanned site does with its own server logs is up to that site.

Changes and questions

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions or concerns: reach us through the GitHub project.

Terms

Terms of Use

Short version: only scan websites you own or have permission to test, and treat the report as a helpful automated check — not a guarantee.

Only scan with permission

You may only use SiteGuard against websites you own, or whose owner has given you permission to test. The VAPT checks actively probe the target site. Testing someone else's site without permission can be illegal where you live — you are responsible for every address you submit.

What the report is

SiteGuard is an automated, non-intrusive check of publicly visible information. It is provided "as is", with no warranty. A clean report does not mean a site is safe — the scanner can miss problems, and it can occasionally flag something that turns out to be fine. It is not a substitute for a professional security assessment.

Fair use

Scans are limited to 10 per hour so the free service stays usable for everyone. Don't try to disrupt SiteGuard or any scanned site, and don't use reports to misrepresent a site you don't control.

Liability

To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for decisions made or losses suffered based on a SiteGuard report. Checking and securing your website remains your responsibility.

Changes

These terms may be updated; the current version always lives on this page. Continuing to use SiteGuard after a change means you accept the updated terms.
